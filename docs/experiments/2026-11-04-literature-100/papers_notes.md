# 100-paper collection: scope and actionable abstract reads

Checked 2026-11-04. The collection contains exactly **100 distinct papers**, with 100 unique canonical DOI/arXiv keys and no exact normalized-title duplicates. **All 99 retrieved original abstracts were read.** P095 has no conventional abstract in the indexed record; its original publisher summary and guideline passages were read instead and are labeled separately. Selected full-text passages were read for 10 records, including nine of the ten actionable papers below. These are not 100 full-text reviews or 100 model replications. No paper's model, dataset, or checkpoint was executed by this collection task.

The public bibliography is [papers.csv](papers.csv) or [papers.json](papers.json), with counts in [papers_summary.json](papers_summary.json), search provenance in [papers_query_log.json](papers_query_log.json), exclusions in [papers_exclusions.json](papers_exclusions.json), and date/duplicate checks in [papers_validation.json](papers_validation.json). Each record supplies a primary DOI/arXiv link, indexed title/year/identifiers, screening level, a short original HyunOptic check implication, proposed test-family mappings, and qualified access evidence. These applications are our inferences from the sources, not author validation of HyunOptic. Every execution status is `not_author_model_reproduction`.

Selection was purposive across optic disc/cup segmentation (35), domain robustness (25), annotation/readers (10), uncertainty/calibration (20), metrics/reporting (8), and foundational robustness (2). EuropePMC queries supplied original-publication abstract metadata; two original arXiv pages supplied the general robustness papers. This is not a systematic review, exhaustive bibliography, study-quality assessment, or performance ranking. Most selected biomedical records are recent because of the API result ordering and the chosen screening pools.

All 100 were absent from the scanned pre-existing repository docs and README by DOI, PMID, or exact normalized title. That is a repository overlap screen, not a claim that the papers are previously unknown. Published EuropePMC MED records were selected, and paired preprints were not counted separately. For Hendrycks and Dietterich, arXiv:1903.12261 supersedes arXiv:1807.01697; only the former is counted. Different titles without shared identifiers can evade automated version matching.

Initial metadata access evidence was limited: 27 records had an indexed EuropePMC full-text link, 5 another indexed free/OA link, 66 publisher links with access unverified, and 2 observed arXiv PDF links. Those counts describe initial metadata evidence. Later successful passage access is recorded separately in each paper's `fulltext_passage_evidence`; it does not turn indexed access labels into verified licenses. Twenty-eight records contain an author-declared resource link in an abstract or arXiv comments. These links do not establish a usable dataset, current license, available weights, or reproducible environment; no checkpoint availability is asserted. All 99 retrieved abstracts were read in batches and kept in ignored local reports. Full abstracts and raw API metadata are not republished here.

## Ten actionable papers

The first table contains brief original abstract paraphrases. The second records additional selected full-text passages actually read. Applications to HyunOptic are proposed checks and do not transfer the papers' validation claims.

| ID | Original source | Abstract observation | Concrete use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P008 | [Multi-dataset, multi-seed preprocessing evaluation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42739310/) | The authors compare five preprocessing pipelines on three OD/OC datasets over three seeds. They explicitly disclose that one crop setting uses reference centroids. | Record seeds and preprocessing exactly; keep reference-derived cropping out of automated deployment claims. Their ranking does not establish a benefit for our frozen models. |
| P021 | [Smartphone ophthalmoscope cup/disc segmentation](https://doi.org/10.3390/vision9020032) | The study uses smartphone-coupled ophthalmoscope images and a dual-decoder architecture, reporting disc and cup overlap separately. | Add acquisition-device metadata to future external evaluation. Synthetic corruption of existing fundus photographs is not smartphone-domain validation. |
| P062 | [Diagnosis-first inter-observer segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2024.3394045) | The authors fuse reader annotations using a downstream diagnosis criterion and include fundus OD/OC among the evaluated tasks. | Preserve reader identities and describe how references are constructed. Diagnosis-informed fusion is a different method from our disagreement score and must not leak test diagnostic labels. |
| P063 | [Multi-rater Prism](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scib.2024.06.037) | The proposed recurrent procedure alternates calibrated segmentation and reader confidence maps. Its abstract links the author code repository. | Separate reader confidence from two-model disagreement. The [author-declared code link](https://github.com/WuJunde/MrPrism) is recorded as an access lead; its assets, license and environment were not verified or run. |
| P079 | [Evaluation of uncertainty estimation methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compmedimag.2025.102574) | The paper distinguishes pixel, sample and model evaluation, proposing corresponding error/calibration measures and a simulated workflow. | Evaluate review priority at the image level against actual reference errors; avoid calling hard-mask disagreement a calibrated probability. Their proposed metrics need probability outputs and their own definitions. |
| P081 | [Failure detection and confidence aggregation benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2024.103392) | The benchmark compares failure detection under test-time shifts, favors risk-coverage analysis, and identifies aggregation of pixel confidence as consequential. Pairwise ensemble Dice is a strong baseline in their study. | Measure retained-case error across coverage and audit low-disagreement/high-error cases. Their five 3D collections do not establish performance for this two-model 2D fundus application. |
| P091 | [Understanding metric-related pitfalls](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-02150-0) | An expert-consensus process organizes limitations and pitfalls of image-analysis validation metrics. | Make undefined geometry, empty masks, aggregation and failure denominators explicit. A metric checklist cannot establish clinical validity. This is distinct from the previously cited Metrics Reloaded companion paper. |
| P097 | [OCT split leakage](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01618-6) | Similar neighboring OCT slices can span an improper train/test split; the study demonstrates inflated classification performance across three datasets. | Keep patient/acquisition groups disjoint and detect related-image duplication. Its classification inflation estimates are not error estimates for our segmentation application. |
| P099 | [Common corruptions and perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.12261) | ImageNet-C and ImageNet-P establish classification benchmarks for common corruptions and perturbations. The arXiv comments link author-hosted dataset resources. | Predeclare corruption families, severity and denominators for the own-model audit. HyunOptic transformations are an inspired local stress protocol, not ImageNet-C/P replication. |
| P100 | [Uncertainty under dataset shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02530) | The authors compare uncertainty methods under classification dataset shift; conventional post-hoc calibration can deteriorate, while some model-marginalization methods perform well. | Check disagreement versus actual error for each shift severity. Clean-data agreement does not prove calibrated confidence or reliability after corruption; classification results do not guarantee segmentation behavior. |

| ID | Additional primary passage read | Further check implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P008 | [Published full text, discussion/conclusions](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC13565358): the authors limit the preprocessing results to one backbone and request per-image scores for proper paired testing. | Preserve paired case scores and avoid treating seed SD as patient-level uncertainty or universal evidence for CLAHE. |
| P021 | [Published methods 3.1–3.2](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC12015843): BrG includes bilateral photographs; cases lacking cup labels are excluded. The preprocessing passage contains inconsistent normalization wording. | Verify patient grouping and missing-label denominators from actual files. Do not infer an exact runnable normalization recipe from this text alone. |
| P062 | [Original preprint 4.1–4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.03016): REFUGE-2 has reader contours; the diagnosis network is pretrained on the training set and then frozen. | Explicitly track diagnostic labels, reader contours and split roles. This related preprint is a different version from the final TMI publication; final-version implementation details remain unchecked. |
| P063 | [Author-hosted published PDF, introduction/methods](https://drliuqi.github.io/files/publications/ConP.pdf): the journal article extends earlier conference work with shuffled supervision and richer experiments. | Keep the journal DOI as the single canonical record; an older arXiv implementation may omit journal additions. |
| P079 | [Publisher access attempt](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0895611125000837) returned HTTP 403; the indexed original abstract was read. | Retain abstract-only status; metric formulas and complete experimental protocol are not independently verified. |
| P081 | [Original preprint 4.4.3 and 5.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.03323): pairwise mask Dice is averaged across prediction pairs; the paper also shows agreeing models that miss an error. | Audit silent failure separately from average ranking performance; disagreement is a confidence proxy whose absolute value need not equal reference Dice. |
| P091 | [Published full text P2.3/P2.4/P3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11181963/): empty masks can yield undefined metrics, and per-image averaging can hide patient clustering. | Preserve explicit undefined states and patient units when aggregating repeated corruptions. |
| P097 | [Published discussion/methods](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC9500039/): downloaded dataset versions differ in subject overlap; normalization and augmentation can introduce additional leakage. | Hash and version split manifests; compute learned normalization only from training data and group augmented relatives together. |
| P099 | [Original full text 4.1–4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.12261v1): the benchmark uses fixed corruption severities on validation images and reserves these corruptions for testing. | Freeze the local corruption protocol before inference; the own-model grid uses different fundus data, severities and metrics. |
| P100 | [Original full text section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.02530v2): methods' uncertainty quality degrades under stronger shifts, and clean-set calibration rankings need not persist. | Report error alongside review priority by corruption severity; two hard-mask predictions cannot support probability-calibration claims. |

P095 is an additional publisher-passages read, outside the actionable-ten table: the [CLAIM 2024 original guideline](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/ryai.240300) distinguishes reference standards from assumed truth, clarifies internal versus external testing, and permits justified not-applicable checklist items. Its short publisher summary replaces a missing conventional abstract in our screening log.

The next reproducibility step for a selected method would be complete full-text and supplement review, confirmed data permission/license, verified author implementation and checkpoint identity, compatible OD/OC references, and a predeclared independent evaluation. None of those steps is implied by inclusion in this bibliography.
