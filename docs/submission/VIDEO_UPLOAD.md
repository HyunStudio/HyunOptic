## Suggested description

HyunOptic is independent research software for inspecting two frozen image models, recording unresolved review decisions and exporting traceable evidence locally.

This demonstration shows actual application captures: session creation, real CPU inference, full-image/model overlays, a held decision, reopening and verified evidence export. Timing is edited; inputs and models are synthetic; narration is computer-generated. Synthetic execution does not establish medical accuracy or clinical benefit. Research only — not for diagnosis or patient care.

## Supported-host publication checklist

Devpost's [official help](https://help.devpost.com/article/85-uploading-a-demo-video) requires a supported video-host URL for its demo field; a GitHub raw MP4 URL does not substitute for that field.

1. Use the submitter's own YouTube, Vimeo or other currently accepted account; check event-specific restrictions.
2. Upload the MP4, title/description and optional thumbnail; add the English SRT.
3. Complete audience, rights and publication settings truthfully. Enable the public viewing and embedding settings required by the event.
4. Open the actual share URL signed out, play the full video and confirm readable captions and sound. Check that Devpost accepts and embeds it.
5. Enter that exact URL in the final submission and record it alongside the submitted source commit. Do not invent a URL or call the GitHub MP4 a hosted-platform upload.

Public GitHub distribution is prepared independently of the submitter-controlled video account. This file records no account login, upload or legal attestation that has not actually occurred.
